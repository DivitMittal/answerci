# Ethics

AnswerCI is for measurement, not manipulation.

Use it to understand whether documentation changes make answer engines more accurate, better cited, and less misleading. Do not use it to mass-generate spam content, stuff pages with adversarial keywords, or pressure providers into ranking a project higher.

Principles:

- Respect provider terms of service.
- Respect robots, rate limits, and human review processes.
- Prefer better documentation over synthetic content farms.
- Measure competitors to understand context, not to impersonate or attack them.
- Keep CI captures deterministic where possible and avoid unnecessary live provider traffic.
